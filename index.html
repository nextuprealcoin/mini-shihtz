Why Imperial Shih Tzus?

Imperial Shih Tzus are known for their compact size, expressive eyes, and affectionate nature. Their lineage traces back to ancient Chinese royalty, and today they remain one of the most beloved companion breeds for families seeking elegance, loyalty, and charm in a tiny package.
